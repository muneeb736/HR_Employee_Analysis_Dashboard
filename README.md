# HR Employee Attrition Dashboard

An Excel-based HR analytics dashboard that explains why employees leave a company. Built on the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset (Kaggle, 1,470 employees).

## Key Questions Answered
- Which department and job role have the highest attrition?
- Which age group leaves the most?
- Does overtime, work-life balance or job satisfaction affect attrition?

## Dashboard Includes
- KPIs: Total Employees, Attrition Count, Average Monthly Income, Average Age, Average Years at Company
- Charts: Attrition by Department, Job Role and Age Group; Attrition Rate by Overtime, Work-Life Balance and Job Satisfaction
- Overtime comparison table
- Department slicer for interactive filtering

## Key Insights
- Research & Development has the highest number of employees leaving.
- Employees working overtime leave far more often than those who do not.
- The 26-35 age group shows the highest attrition.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel: Power Query, Pivot Tables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Formulas
